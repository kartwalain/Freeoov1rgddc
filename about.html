About FreeOO AI PRO
Free. Real-Time. Built for Indian Option Traders.
FreeOO AI PRO is a completely free, professional-grade, real-time options analytics dashboard built specifically for retail derivatives traders in India's NSE and BSE markets. Powered by Upstox API v3 WebSocket, delivering true tick-by-tick live data for Nifty 50 and Sensex with zero lag — directly in your browser, on mobile, tablet, or desktop.

About FreeOO.in

FreeOO.in is a Mumbai-based fintech initiative founded with one clear mission — to level the playing field for retail investors and traders in India.

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in Indian equity and derivatives markets, we understand the challenges retail traders face every single day: expensive tools, delayed data, complex analytics, and market insights that were previously available only to institutional players.

That's why we built FreeOO AI PRO — a completely free, professional-grade, real-time options analytics dashboard for NSE and BSE markets. No subscriptions. No paywalls. Just powerful data in your hands.

What FreeOO AI PRO Delivers

⚡ Live Option Chain
Full Nifty & Sensex option chain with real-time OI, Change in OI, LTP, Delta, Theta, IV%, and multi-timeframe OI delta columns (3m / 10m / 30m Δ OI) to instantly spot where smart money is building or exiting positions.
📊 PCR & Max Pain
Real-time Put-Call Ratio and Max Pain for every expiry, helping you gauge overall market sentiment and identify where the index is likely to gravitate by expiry.
🎯 Net Gamma Exposure (GEX)
Live Nifty Net GEX with market regime detection, so traders understand whether dealers are in a positive gamma (market-dampening) or negative gamma (market-amplifying) environment.
📈 India VIX & ATM IV
Track fear and implied volatility in real time for both Nifty and Sensex ATM strikes — critical for option premium buyers and sellers alike.
🕯️ Candlestick Charts with VWAP & OI Overlay
Intraday price charts (1m/5m/15m/30m) with VWAP, Open Interest histogram, and Change-in-OI overlay for technical confluence with derivatives data.
🔟 10-Strike Option Chain Summary
Compact 5 ITM + ATM + 5 OTM view across all expiries for both Nifty and Sensex, giving you a fast expiry-to-expiry comparison at a glance.
🤖 Multi-Expiry Strike Recommendations
AI-assisted support/resistance levels, strike suggestions, and gamma-based target zones to aid entry and exit decisions.
🌐 Global Market Hub
Quick-access links to TradingView, SensiBull, NiftyTrader, Economic Calendar, World Indices, Forex, and more — all from one dashboard.
